About the platform

One platform for every college event

The College Event Management System is an online platform that enables students to discover, register, and participate in college events. Administrators can efficiently manage events, registrations, payments, attendance, and reports through a centralized dashboard.

Built for two audiences
For students

Discover, register, attend

  • Browse Technical, Cultural, Sports and Workshop events in one place
  • Register in a few clicks with secure online payment
  • Track every registration from a personal dashboard
  • Get a digital ticket with seat and token details
For administrators

Manage everything, centrally

  • Create and edit events, seats and registration fees
  • Monitor registrations and payment status in real time
  • Mark attendance and generate participation reports
  • Review incoming messages from the contact page
How it works
01
